Jigsaw by Pagouro

Relaxing Belle Époque jigsaws. No ads, nothing to buy, nothing locked.

A quiet, free jigsaw puzzle game of Belle Époque posters: poplars at sunset, the Eiffel Tower in spring, irises in a tall vase, balloons over Paris rooftops, a black cat on a red cushion. A hundred and sixty pictures, every one drawn by our own AI picture model.

Android, in testing on Google Play now. The public release follows the test. This page will carry the Play Store link the day it does.

Free, and nothing else

  • No ads at all, ever. Nothing to buy, no coins, no subscription, nothing locked: all 160 pictures are free from the first day.
  • No account, and the app collects no data (privacy policy). It works fully offline and asks for no permissions.

The puzzles

  • A daily puzzle: the same picture for everyone each day, at the size you like.
  • Any of the 160 pictures at 12, 25, 49, 100 or 156 pieces.
  • Two cuts: Whimsical (waving edges, bulbs, caps and arrowheads) or Classic (a traditional die-cut look).
  • Optional rotation for experts: pieces start turned; tap to turn.
  • A gentle 62-minute piano loop in the style of the Paris salon, by Pagouro Salon, our small AI music generator trained only on public-domain scores.

Finish the daily and tap Share to copy your result for the family chat, like this:

The number is the day's puzzle, so everyone posting the same number did the same picture. It never names the picture, so nobody's puzzle gets spoiled.

About the AI pictures

We want to be plain about this. The pictures were drawn by Pagouro BE, our own AI picture generator. It learned its Belle Époque style from public-domain posters made before 1929, each listed with its source in a public ledger, on top of an open base model trained on Creative Commons-licensed images. Its sources, including the parts we did not train ourselves, are documented on the front page and in its repository. The 160 pictures in the game were picked by hand from more than 1,400 drawings.
